For Ibrahem;
A swollen canary;
His mind;
His home demolished, a ripe olive, shriveled with fear;
Who will live on his home?
For Ibrahem;
I dedicate this;
An acknowledgement of his loss;
A hope and wish that he finds home again;
He is not my canary;
Let him fly freely;
Protect his flight from the specter which attempts to domesticate him;
Let him not erode;
May his name be etched in his home;
Where he cared for his mother;
*
His father;
*
His sisters;
*
His wife and children;
*
May they be happy where they are;
Please gift them a peace we may never find;
A peace found in nullity;
